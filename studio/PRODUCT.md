# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Delegated by the user. One-page HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript for the selected Web on Demand builder. Web Audio for processing; native WebGL for the 3D character. Separate local Node service for GPT-5.6 Luna conversation, with bounded estimated spend; existing local Ollama remains optional.

## Users
The Hybrid Elite Mafia creator, importing music stems and preparing mixes.

## Product Purpose
A working DAW with custom effect creation and the embedded Ghost in the Machine assistant.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Import and synchronized playback, waveform arrangement, mixing, trim and split, 18 original vocal/mastering/repair processors, modular effect chains, 94 opt-in presets, zero/bypass defaults, a master bus, A/B, automation, MIDI learn, estimated output meters, local projects, and WAV mixdown. Model-based conversation requires a separate service. A procedural 3D interpretation does not reproduce every detail of the supplied 2D artwork. Browser effect chains are not VST or AU binaries. No claim of automatic distribution acceptance or certified mastering.

## Brand Commitments
Ghost in the Machine; Hybrid Elite Mafia; black, cyan, violet; dimensional controls; the supplied humanoid artwork. User delegated the remaining format and design decisions.

## Evidence on Hand
The uploaded character image, displayed as an artwork option. No uploaded audio stems yet.

## Product Principles
Audio controls must affect audio. Measure before suggesting changes. Keep costs visible and do not call paid AI services automatically. Clearly distinguish connected AI from the local guide.


## Studio tools update
The local edition now includes an original MIDI piano roll and basic wavetable instrument, keyboard and Web MIDI note input, browser audio recording, clip fades/reverse, track automation, time ranges, aligned stem/MIDI/float-WAV exports, redo and saved chat. Suno's private model functionality, advanced recording/editor features and conversational arbitrary plugin authoring are not reproduced. The research report records those gaps; no full Suno parity or hosted deployment is claimed.
