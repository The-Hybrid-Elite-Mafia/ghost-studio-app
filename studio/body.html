    Privacy and usage limits
    Your key is encrypted on Ghost’s server for your signed-in account. It is never saved in browser storage or included in a project.
    Chat and lyrics use GPT-5.6 Luna with a shared $5 monthly estimated-spend limit and 100 requests per day for this online app. Other apps and your PC copy have separate usage.
    Only chat, submitted lyrics, track names, settings and measurements are sent. Songs, stems and projects stay on the device where you load them. Playback and supported cleanup commands work without API charges.
    Removing the saved key disconnects this online Ghost account. It does not revoke the key at OpenAI or reset your usage.
    

    

    Your studio guide
    Open the full illustrated manual ↗ — labeled control diagrams, step-by-step instructions and every plugin explained.
    Make a mix
    Use Import song / stems for an actual WAV/MP3 song or aligned audio stems, including vocals. Import MIDI notes loads instrument notes without the original recording or singer. A MIDI track named “Synth Voice” does not contain sung words. With both audio and MIDI in a session, Solo the audio track to hear it alone. Press Play. Drag channel faders for level and drag knobs vertically for pan or effect parameters. Arrow keys also adjust knobs; double-click resets them. Space plays or pauses while you are not typing.
    Arrange audio
    Drag a waveform clip to move it. Set precise timing and trim points in Track detail. Click the ruler to seek; split the selected stem at the playhead. BPM changes the grid, MIDI note timing and tempo-synced delay. Imported audio remains at its original speed and pitch. Track detail also offers fades, reversal, clip gain, duplication and volume/pan automation. Mute and Solo affect both playback and mixdown.
    Create plugins
    Add modules in Plugin workshop, set their controls, give your chain a name, and save it. Each stem and the master bus has its own effect chain with no fixed plugin-count cap. Larger active chains use more processing power and memory; bypass unused effects or use prepared playback if the device struggles. Insert it on the selected stem, bypass modules in Track detail, and download the plugin to keep it. New plugins start at zero with bypass on. Choose a preset to activate its sound. AudioWorklet racks edit live. In prepared playback, edits prepare in the background and switch automatically without stopping the song. A/B preserves two settings snapshots. These are browser audio effect chains, not VST or AU files.
    Work with Ghost
    Hover or focus a control for help. Analyze stem measures the selected source before effects. Say “apply needed plugins” to measure all audible imported stems, apply justified clean processing, and report the changes. Ghost also accepts “create MIDI track,” “add chord C major,” “set tempo to 120,” “set fade in to 0.02 seconds,” “rename all tracks by role,” and “create a plugin called Clear Voice with EQ and de-esser.” Add “to the selected stem” for one track. Undo reverses the operation. DAW commands work with or without a connected model. The local guide understands supported questions and commands; it is not a general AI model. The included service connects GPT-5.6 Luna for wider conversation, with estimated monthly and daily usage limits. Opening the app or checking the connection does not generate a paid reply. Supported DAW commands remain local. The model receives text and measurements; it does not hear or generate songs.
    Character and motion
    Ghost is an actual articulated geometric 3D interpretation inspired by your image. It is not an exact reconstruction. Drag to orbit; switch to Artwork to see your supplied image. The knobs and desk use CSS depth. Motion can be switched off.
    Save and export
    Save keeps one session, including chat, in this browser. Download project backup includes the audio and edits for another session. Export renders stereo 16/24-bit PCM or 32-bit float WAV at 44.1 or 48 kHz, plus aligned stem archives and MIDI note files. A time range can limit an audio export. Stem archives exclude the master bus and preserve each track’s edits. Export estimates intersample peaks with 4× interpolation and reduces gain if needed for your chosen ceiling, with 0.1 dB of extra margin. Live mastering meters show estimated momentary loudness and true peak; they are not certified meters.
    Audio loading
    If the audio module cannot load, Automatic prepares audio before playing it. You can also choose Prepared playback under Audio mode & troubleshooting. Stem Cleanup always uses this path to preserve alignment. Large stem sets also use prepared playback to reduce memory use. Preparation progress appears below the transport; Stop cancels it. Preparation needs time and memory. Live insert meters are unavailable in this mode; the master sample-peak meter remains active.
    Browser edition limits
    Up to 24 tracks, 160 MB per imported audio file and 12-minute stems. Large sets keep their original files and prepare one stem at a time for playback, instead of keeping every decoded stem in memory. A 96 MB decoded-file cache and a 512 MB preparation guard leave room for processing. Very long clips or heavy cleanup may still need shorter clips. Save and project backups include the original audio; the browser’s storage quota still applies. Supported codecs depend on your browser. Audio-input and MIDI recording, a piano roll, a basic wavetable instrument, clip fades/reverse, time ranges, volume/pan automation and Undo/Redo are included. This edition does not host VST/AU plugins, separate stems, independently time-stretch/pitch-shift audio, or generate songs. Loop recording, comp lanes and input latency calibration are not implemented. Automation and MIDI learn are available on the plugin processors; cleanup settings are rendered before playback. A professional release still needs listening checks and the destination’s current delivery requirements.
